## 5. Material deltas

The PASS does not depend on claiming that AI changes every cluster. Important material deltas are:

1. **Broader Veles family** — Alice directly observes a multi-form selection/suitability job. Pass B therefore authorizes a Veles-family selection/explanation asset even while a separate commercial category remains conditional on SKU depth.
2. **Alatyr** — ordinary Search supports commerce; Alice is meaning/mythology/suitability-first with a large mixed informational/content-commerce source set. The owned page job therefore becomes substantively hybrid content-commerce rather than a thin commercial landing with incidental meaning copy.
3. **Vegvisir** — ordinary Search already exposed a meaning split, but Alice makes historical explanation/correction the dominant job on the broad high-demand root itself. The meaning/history asset moves from P1 to launch-critical P0 and requires explicit provenance/accuracy.
4. **Gift contamination correction** — a context-contaminated Alice run injected runic/Veles suggestions because of prior conversation; a clean rerun did not. Preserving provenance prevents a false generic-gift architecture expansion.
5. **Specialist source gap** — Search proves specialist sites can rank; Alice additionally proves specialist commerce-content pages can be answer sources. The owned-site requirement becomes ranking + source-worthiness, not ranking alone.

## 6. Important NO_CHANGE findings
