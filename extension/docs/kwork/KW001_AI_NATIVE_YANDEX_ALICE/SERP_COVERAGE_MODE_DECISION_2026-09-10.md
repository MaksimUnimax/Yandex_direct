## Why

KW-001 is an existing-site AI-native semantic rebuild, not a standalone promise of full TOP-based clustering for every active phrase.

The accepted current KW-001 method already uses ordinary Search as direct evidence for material intent, duplicate, result-type, page-boundary and architecture questions while preserving exact observation scope and unresolved states.

Historical/current method evidence includes:
- bounded Step09 Search tranche;
- task-first clustering;
- later focused Search for ownership/structural boundaries;
- bounded AI diagnostic selection rather than bulk AI execution.

Therefore a normal KW-001 order follows the frozen selective mode. It does not reopen the portfolio-method choice from scratch.

## Execution boundary

Every governed phrase still receives the semantic/accounting treatment required by the KW-001 roadmap.

Search manifest must be derived from real product decisions, for example:

```text
material semantic ambiguity
split/merge boundary
non-exact duplicate boundary
result/page-type uncertainty
page ownership uncertainty
important architecture contrast/control
later competing-page case where fresh current Search is material
```

Unprobed phrases are not called directly Search-confirmed.

## Re-open trigger

Re-open only for a versioned/material product change such as:
- converting KW-001 to a full-TOP clustering promise;
- adding a new greenfield mode;
- materially changing provider/transport/economics in a way that changes the sold evidence depth;
- owner-directed product version reopen.

The KW-002 full-SERP decision does not automatically change KW-001.
