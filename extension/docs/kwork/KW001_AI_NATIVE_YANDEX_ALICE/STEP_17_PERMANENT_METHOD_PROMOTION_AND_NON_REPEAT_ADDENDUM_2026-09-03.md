## Permanent failure lesson

The reusable failure was not simply "too few AI queries". The deeper process defect was:

```text
A LIMITATION COULD BE DISCLOSED
WITHOUT CHANGING
THE ALLOWED CLAIM / CONFIDENCE / PASS GATE.
```

That allowed a technically complete case matrix to look stronger than its AI sampling actually supported.

Permanent correction:

```text
LIMITATION
-> CLAIM SCOPE
-> CONFIDENCE STATE
-> CONFIRMATION / RECHECK TRIGGER
-> PASS CONSEQUENCE
```

If that chain is absent, the limitation is not governed and Step17 cannot final-pass.
