
User jobs observed: buy, compare profiles, estimate price, choose by performance/budget.

Directly read representatives include Blitz, Thermo, Grazio and Intelio. Additional model names appear in the category structure. This is a real product-profile family, not merely one category page.

### F03 — window use-case/design segmentation

### F04 — windows by house series

### F09 — accessories/customisation/security

Directly read representatives include screens, drip caps, blinds, anti-burglary hardware, lamination, decorative bars and handles. The Work pass also observed linked glass-option families. These are meaningful accessory/benefit jobs, not only footer noise.

These have different jobs and must not be collapsed into one generic service intent before demand/SERP evidence.

### F11 — price / calculators / conversion utilities

Directly read price and two calculator-style URLs. They support estimate/configuration jobs but do not themselves prove external query demand.

### F12 — trust/company/source assets

Directly read company, production, reviews and contacts. They provide supplier/process/source-worthiness evidence and can also participate in manufacturer/trust intents.

### F13 — informational/editorial content

Directly read article hub plus guides on choosing windows, coloured windows, panoramic glazing, balcony finishing, glass units and micro-ventilation. The article family is therefore substantial enough that later AI recommendations must first check whether an explanatory job already has an owned asset.

### F14 — GEO
