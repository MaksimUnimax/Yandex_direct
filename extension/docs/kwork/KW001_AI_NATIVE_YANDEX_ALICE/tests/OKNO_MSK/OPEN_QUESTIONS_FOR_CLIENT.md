# KW-001 / OKNO-MSK — OPEN QUESTIONS FOR CLIENT

Date: 2026-08-28
Status: STEP 01 CLIENT-CLARIFICATION SET

These questions are intentionally limited to facts that public-site review cannot establish reliably. In a real Kwork order they should be asked before expensive provider acquisition when the answer can materially change scope or interpretation.

## Must-ask before semantic acquisition

1. **Primary commercial region:** is the base order Moscow city only, Moscow + Moscow region, or selected cities/districts as separate priorities?
2. **Priority business families:** which of the following matter most commercially right now: standard PVC windows, specific REHAU profiles, doors, balconies/loggias, verandas, aluminium glazing, installation?
3. **Actual product availability:** are all profile systems and service variants shown on the site still actively sold and strategically relevant?
4. **Independent installation:** do you actively sell window installation as a standalone service for windows purchased elsewhere, or is installation mainly part of your own-window package?
5. **Geographic pages:** are city subdomains and district landing pages an intentional current strategy that must be preserved, or are they open to consolidation if evidence suggests overlap?
6. **New-page constraint:** may the recommendation include new pages/categories, or must work be limited to existing URLs?
7. **Page removal/merge constraint:** may the recommendation include merging or retiring existing pages if strong evidence supports it?
8. **Commercial exclusions:** are there products, profile systems, service types, districts/cities or customer segments that should not be promoted even if search demand exists?

## Useful commercial-priority questions

9. Which directions have the best margin / operational capacity, if the client is willing to share this qualitatively (`high / medium / low`) rather than exact financial data?
10. Are there directions currently overloaded with demand where more traffic is not desired?
11. Are there strategic directions the business wants to grow even if current search demand is smaller?
12. Are B2B/commercial-building orders in scope, or should the analysis focus on residential B2C?

## Content / AI questions

13. Can existing commercial pages be substantially rewritten/expanded, or are there CMS/legal/brand limitations?
14. Can new expert/selection materials be created when evidence justifies them?
15. Is the client specifically interested in stronger presence as an AI answer source, or is AI evidence being used primarily to improve ordinary page architecture and user coverage?
16. Are there claims that must not be used in content without internal technical/legal approval (warranty lengths, thermal/noise values, installation standards, manufacturer status, etc.)?

## Optional access questions for later upsell — not required for KW-001 base package

17. Is Yandex Webmaster access available?
18. Is Yandex Metrika access available?
19. Is Yandex Direct data available and relevant?

Base KW-001 must remain deliverable if the answer to 17–19 is NO.
