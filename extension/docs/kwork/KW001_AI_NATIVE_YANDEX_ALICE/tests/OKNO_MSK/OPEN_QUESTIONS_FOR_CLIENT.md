# KW-001 / OKNO-MSK — OPEN QUESTIONS FOR CLIENT

Date: 2026-08-28  
Status: **UPDATED AFTER MERGED STEP-01 DISCOVERY**

These questions are limited to facts that public-site discovery cannot establish reliably and that can materially change semantic scope, prioritization or interpretation.

## Must-ask before semantic acquisition

1. **Primary commercial region:** Moscow city only, Moscow + Moscow region, or selected cities/districts as separate priorities?
2. **Priority business families:** which directions matter most commercially now: broad PVC windows, particular REHAU profiles, doors, balconies/loggias, verandas/outbuildings, aluminium glazing, installation, repair/service, accessories?
3. **Current active assortment:** are all visible profile systems, door types, glazing variants and service pages still active offers rather than legacy content?
4. **Residential vs B2B:** should the work focus on residential B2C, or are office/commercial-building glazing jobs materially in scope?
5. **Standalone installation:** do you actively sell installation for windows purchased elsewhere, or is installation mainly bundled with your own products?
6. **Repair/service acquisition:** is repair of existing/third-party windows an active acquisition direction, or mainly after-sales support?
7. **Accessories:** are screens, blinds, security hardware, lamination, handles and similar products strategic standalone acquisition directions or mainly upsells?
8. **Finance/instalments:** is instalment/credit traffic an acquisition priority or only supporting conversion information?
9. **Geographic architecture:** are district pages and city subdomains intentional current acquisition assets that must be preserved, or are they open to evidence-led consolidation/reassignment?
10. **New pages:** may recommendations include new pages/categories when evidence justifies them?
11. **Merge/retirement:** may recommendations include merging, redirecting or retiring existing pages if strong evidence later supports it?
12. **Commercial exclusions:** are there products, profile systems, service types, cities/districts or customer segments that should not be promoted even when demand exists?

## Useful commercial-priority questions

13. Which directions have the best margin/capacity, if the client can share this qualitatively (`high / medium / low`)?
14. Are any directions already overloaded, where more traffic is not desired?
15. Are there strategic directions the company wants to grow even if current search demand is smaller?
16. Which conversion path is commercially preferred: calculator, free measurement, phone/callback, or another route?
17. Are house-series pages a deliberate lead-generation strategy supported by sales experience, or mainly a content/catalog convenience?
18. Are GEO pages/subdomains operated with meaningful local service differences, or mainly localized versions of the same offer?

## Content / AI questions

19. Can existing commercial pages be substantially rewritten/expanded, or are there CMS/legal/brand constraints?
20. Can new expert/selection materials be created when evidence justifies them?
21. Are existing articles actively maintained and available for substantial revision if they are better targets than new URLs?
22. Is the client specifically interested in stronger presence as an AI-answer source, or is AI evidence primarily being used to improve ordinary page architecture and user coverage?
23. Are there technical/legal claims that require internal approval before use: warranty terms, profile characteristics, installation standards, manufacturer status, etc.?
