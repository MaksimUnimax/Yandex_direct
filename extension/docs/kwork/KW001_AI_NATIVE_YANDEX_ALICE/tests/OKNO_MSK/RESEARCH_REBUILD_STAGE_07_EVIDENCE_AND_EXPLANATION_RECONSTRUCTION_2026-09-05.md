## What ordinary Yandex Search proved

The preserved Search corpus was used to identify the job expressed by exact queries, dominant landing/result type, commercial-versus-informational character, query boundaries and page-role evidence. It materially supported:

- separating accessory/catalog shopping from generic window-service demand;
- separating open-balcony finishing from glazing;
- preserving use-case-first ownership for balcony, veranda and private-house tasks where observed;
- distinguishing commercial pages from explanatory/support pages;
- refusing destructive consolidation where Search showed related responsibilities rather than proven harm.

Each Step 09 observation remains bounded to its exact query. A normalized projection is not treated as the raw provider body. An unprobed phrase or entire family is not described as observed merely because a nearby representative query was checked.

## What Search did not prove

Search evidence does not by itself prove traffic, conversion, historical cannibalization harm, stable long-term rank, business availability or whole-family coverage. Absence from a result set is not site absence. These limits remain attached to each row and to the final narrative.

## AI decision delta

AI did not produce an architecture change in any of the eight preregistered cases.

- **DE_RISK (4):** reinforced the Search-only owner/structure while sometimes exposing bounded within-page content depth.
- **NO_CHANGE (3):** did not justify a new action; one case retained current ambiguity rather than forcing certainty.
- **INSUFFICIENT (1):** did not support an architecture conclusion, but allowed a bounded freshness/methodology content action.

This is still analytical value: a supported no-change or de-risk result prevents unjustified restructuring. It is not represented as the fact that an AI request occurred.
