## Important structural observations — hypotheses only

These are not SEO verdicts. They identify boundaries that later demand/SERP/AI evidence must resolve.

1. `homepage` vs `/okna-rehau/` both cover broad window purchase and REHAU selection, so broad-root ownership must be measured.
2. The REHAU category already contains extensive comparison and FAQ material while separate profile pages and an informational `how to choose` article also exist. Search/AI evidence must determine the right division between commercial selection and editorial explanation.
3. Balcony architecture is the most complex family: glazing mode, physical shape, house series, district, finishing and special solutions all create separate URLs.
4. `aluminium windows` overlaps several cold-glazing use cases. Material-led intent and object/use-case intent should not be assumed equivalent.
5. Veranda/gazebo/terrace are combined at hub level, while warm/cold children exist. Demand and SERP may justify a different split.
6. Geo architecture exists in two forms: main-domain district pages and city subdomains. Step 01 cannot determine their semantic necessity.
7. `production` and broad commercial pages all make manufacturer/own-production claims; later Search evidence should determine whether `от производителя` is a separate landing job or supporting trust proof.
8. The site already has source-worthy informational assets and dense FAQ blocks, which makes it suitable for testing whether AI evidence changes page-job decisions rather than merely suggesting generic FAQ creation.
