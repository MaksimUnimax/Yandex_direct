Why this check matters:

```text

```

A phrase can contain a relevant product term while its actual navigational/brand target is another company. D12-12 therefore does not mean "rescue every window-looking phrase"; it means challenge upstream outside states when later evidence contradicts them and preserve outside scope when the evidence confirms it.
