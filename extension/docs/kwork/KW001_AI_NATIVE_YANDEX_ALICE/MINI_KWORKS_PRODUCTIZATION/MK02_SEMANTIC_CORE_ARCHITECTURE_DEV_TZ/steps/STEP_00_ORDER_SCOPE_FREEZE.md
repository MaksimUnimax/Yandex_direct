# MK02 STEP 00 — ORDER / SCOPE / STRUCTURAL-CONSTRAINT FREEZE

## PURPOSE
Freeze exactly what client site, Yandex region, business directions and architecture/change constraints MK02 is allowed to research before any acquisition begins.

## WHY THIS STEP EXISTS
Later semantic or architectural evidence must not silently redefine the paid task. MK02 can recommend real site changes, so it also needs known protected pages/elements and business constraints recorded before architecture decisions become final.

## INPUTS
Client URL; primary region; business/conversion purpose; included directions; exclusions; known structural/change constraints; optional existing semantic/structure materials; current commercial package capacity when defined.

## REQUIRED EVIDENCE
Client-provided/confirmed order facts; public URL existence; explicit `NONE KNOWN` where client has no known exclusions or structural restrictions.

## METHOD
Create a Level-2 order record before provider work. Distinguish client-stated facts from public-site inference. Freeze the sold product as MK02/Yandex-only. Record protected URLs/forms/calculators/sections or `NONE KNOWN`. Record unresolved material client questions instead of filling them by assumption.

## OUTPUTS
Frozen order/scope record with site, region, business job, included/excluded directions, known protected elements, requested MK02 result and unresolved questions.

## SOURCE KW-001 AUTHORITY
KW-001 Step0 lessons; MK01 Step00; MK02 `PRODUCT_SCOPE.md`; `CLIENT_INPUT_CONTRACT.md`.

## KNOWN FAILURE CLASSES
S01 evidence rewrote scope; M12-13 business truth treated as owner strategy; M12-19 public inference presented like client-confirmed policy.

## ROOT CAUSES
Research convenience and later findings were allowed to substitute for the actual client contract.

## NON-REPEAT CONTROLS
Freeze before acquisition; preserve evidence-source type for business constraints; explicit revision log for material later scope changes.

## CLAIM BOUNDARIES
Unknown technical/business restrictions are not proof that none exist. Public site content is not equivalent to client-confirmed strategic priority.

## UNKNOWN / BLOCKER BEHAVIOR
Missing site/region/business scope blocks normal acquisition. Material unclear structural restriction blocks only the downstream action it can change when the overall research can proceed safely.

## PASS GATE
Public site URL readable; region confirmed; included/excluded directions confirmed; business purpose confirmed; known structural constraints confirmed or `NONE KNOWN`; Yandex-only boundary acknowledged; order freeze persisted.

## CLIENT-FACING MEANING
«До начала сбора фиксируем, что именно исследуем и какие важные элементы сайта нужно учитывать, чтобы итоговая структура и задания не противоречили реальному бизнесу и ограничениям сайта.»
