# MK02 STEP 06 — ROW-LEVEL SEMANTIC CLEANUP

## PURPOSE
Assign an explicit semantic/business state and reason to every governed unique phrase before clustering and architecture begin.

## WHY THIS STEP EXISTS
Architecture cannot safely inherit default KEEP, family-level assumptions or frequency-based relevance. Every active phrase must earn inclusion from positive semantic/business evidence.

## INPUTS
Unioned persisted Wordstat evidence; frozen business scope; current business/site profile; triage history.

## REQUIRED EVIDENCE
Exact phrase; demand/provenance context; business scope; relevant task/object signals; preserved previous screening state.

## METHOD
Normalize exact duplicate phrases without losing occurrence history. Review every governed unique phrase. Use explicit equivalent states for accepted, review, scope exclusion, irrelevance and mechanical/noise cases. KEEP requires positive fit; absence of a rejection rule is insufficient. Preserve ambiguity for Search/deferred routing. Run adversarial/manual semantic QA capable of rejecting automated decisions.

## OUTPUTS
One current row-level decision per governed phrase; explicit reason; preserved uncertainty; exact accounting back to occurrence evidence.

## SOURCE KW-001 AUTHORITY
KW-001 Step7 corrected cleanup method; MK01 Step06; semantic failure ledger.

## KNOWN FAILURE CLASSES
S06 family triage = cleanup; S07 low frequency exclusion; S08 high count/association auto-keep; S10 default KEEP; S11 arithmetic QA = semantic QA.

## ROOT CAUSES
Convenient deterministic rules and clean counts were mistaken for positive semantic proof.

## NON-REPEAT CONTROLS
No default KEEP; frequency never decides alone; review preserved; positive evidence for active inclusion; independent semantic challenge sample; occurrence provenance retained.

## CLAIM BOUNDARIES
Accepted phrase means relevant to the frozen business/task evidence. It does not yet mean a cluster, page owner, structural action or implementation task.

## UNKNOWN / BLOCKER BEHAVIOR
Ambiguous phrases remain REVIEW/deferred/Search-required rather than being forced into the working core for completeness.

## PASS GATE
Every governed phrase accounted exactly once; no silent drops; no default KEEP; low/high frequency shortcuts absent; uncertainty preserved; semantic QA PASS.

## CLIENT-FACING MEANING
«Каждый запрос проверяется отдельно: в рабочее ядро попадает только то, что действительно соответствует бизнесу и задаче пользователя, а спорные фразы не прячутся и не принимаются автоматически.»
