# MK02 STEP 02 — WORDSTAT ACQUISITION PLAN

## PURPOSE
Design a bounded Yandex Wordstat collection plan that covers the frozen business scope without pre-deciding relevance, page ownership or architecture.

## WHY THIS STEP EXISTS
Seeds are acquisition probes. Treating site labels, obvious commercial phrases or current page names as final keywords would bias all later clustering and architecture decisions.

## INPUTS
Frozen order; Step01 business/site vocabulary; existing semantic list if provided; current provider/cost constraints.

## REQUIRED EVIDENCE
Every seed/probe must have a named information purpose tied to a frozen business direction or a specific coverage gap.

## METHOD
Create a manifest of bounded seed requests, region, device/operator mode, expected evidence and stop logic. Use the current site only as vocabulary/business context, not as the final semantic taxonomy. Predeclare how a later second pass may be triggered.

## OUTPUTS
Seed/probe manifest; acquisition parameters; cost/volume guardrails; expected persistence schema; stop criteria.

## SOURCE KW-001 AUTHORITY
KW-001 Step2; MK01 Step02; evidence-quality/provider-cost policy.

## KNOWN FAILURE CLASSES
S03 seed = final keyword; S09 recursive acquisition; S01 evidence rewrites scope.

## ROOT CAUSES
Collection convenience was allowed to determine the future semantic and page model.

## NON-REPEAT CONTROLS
`SEED != FINAL KEYWORD`; each seed has information purpose; no open-ended recursion; no Step5A competitor-derived expansion hidden in MK02 base.

## CLAIM BOUNDARIES
A seed proves neither demand relevance nor page need. Current site taxonomy does not automatically define final clusters or architecture.

## UNKNOWN / BLOCKER BEHAVIOR
If a business direction is too unclear to formulate a truthful acquisition probe, clarify scope rather than inventing adjacent demand.

## PASS GATE
All probes map to frozen scope; region/mode explicit; persistence schema defined; recursion/stop logic explicit; competitor expansion absent from base.

## CLIENT-FACING MEANING
«Планируем сбор так, чтобы охватить согласованные направления, но не подгоняем будущее ядро и структуру сайта под заранее придуманные ключи.»
