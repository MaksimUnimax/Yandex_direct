# MK03 STEP 04 — COMPETITOR-DERIVED TOPIC / SEED HYPOTHESES

## Purpose
Turn genuinely new competitor-page observations into bounded demand probes with explicit lineage.

## Why this step exists
Competitor page topics can reveal blind spots, but are not automatically accepted keywords.

## Inputs
Competitor page evidence; client discovery baseline/current coverage.

## Required evidence
Exact page/topic element motivating the hypothesis plus evidence that the direction is not already adequately represented.

## Method
1. Compare observed competitor topics with existing client baseline/known directions.
2. Create a seed only when a missed-demand/information-gain hypothesis exists.
3. Preserve lineage: seed ← competitor domain ← URL ← exposing Yandex query ← motivating page element.
4. Do not create probes for every heading, SKU or lexical variant.
5. Route obvious duplicates/known directions as already covered before provider expansion where safe.

## Outputs
`COMPETITOR_DERIVED_SEED_REGISTER` with hypothesis and lineage.

## Source authority
Parent Step5A 5A.3 plus volume/sanitation addendum.

## Known failures / root causes
- every heading becomes a seed;
- page phrase becomes accepted keyword;
- seed lacks source lineage;
- recursive expansion continues without novelty.

## Non-repeat controls
`SEED = INFORMATION-GAIN PROBE`; `SEED != FINAL KEYWORD`.

## Claim boundary
A seed expresses a testable demand hypothesis only.

## Unknown behavior
Potentially material but ambiguous directions remain HOLD for business/scope clarification rather than forced provider spend.

## PASS gate
Every active seed has explicit novelty hypothesis and full competitor-page lineage; no heading/SKU explosion.

## Client-facing meaning
“Competitor pages are used to generate testable missing-demand directions, not to copy their wording blindly.”
