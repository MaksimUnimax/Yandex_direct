# MK03 STEP 09 — CLIENT MATERIALIZATION AND QA

## Purpose
Materialize an inspectable client result and verify analytical, accounting, physical and recipient usability.

## Why this step exists
Correct internal tables and counts can still produce a useless or misleading client deliverable.

## Inputs
Competitor discovery/page evidence, seed/provenance, demand funnel, visibility matrix, gap decisions, structural opportunities and unresolved states.

## Required evidence
Final canonical Level-2 authorities plus generators/sources used for client artifacts.

## Method
1. Expose the sold causal chain in plain language.
2. Keep inspectable material competitors/pages/gaps, not only aggregate counts.
3. Distinguish page-topic evidence from tested Search visibility.
4. Show confirmed gaps, already-covered directions, off-scope rejects where useful and HOLD items with exact missing evidence.
5. Explain bounded next opportunities without implementation overclaim.
6. Keep internal IDs/files/Step chronology out of ordinary client prose.
7. Run analytical/accounting QA, recipient task tests and physical artifact QA.
8. Fix source/generator first, regenerate, then inspect final bytes.

## Outputs
Client-facing XLSX/PDF or equivalent physical package defined by `DELIVERABLE_SPEC.md`; QA receipt; release manifest/readback.

## Source authority
Parent Step19/20 packaging/recipient gates; local PRODUCT_SCOPE and GENERAL_RULES.

## Known failures / root causes
- aggregate counts only;
- internal jargon/provenance dumped instead of explanation;
- tested/unprobed distinction lost;
- PDF/XLSX physically exists but is unreadable;
- final binary patched without fixing source;
- correct gaps omitted because no physical action exists.

## Non-repeat controls
Recipient must answer arbitrary material competitor→page→new direction→gap state→opportunity without repository knowledge.

## Claim boundary
Client language reports exactly what evidence supports; no full competitor keyword universe, traffic/revenue estimate, final architecture or implementation-ready TZ in base MK03.

## Unknown behavior
HOLD/SEARCH_REQUIRED items remain visible with exact resolving check; they are not hidden to make the report look complete.

## PASS gate
All QA gates in `QA_AND_RELEASE.md` pass; final artifacts are rendered/inspected; remote readback matches released authorities/artifacts.

## Client-facing meaning
“You receive not just totals, but an inspectable answer showing competitors, missed demand, evidence and what each finding means.”
