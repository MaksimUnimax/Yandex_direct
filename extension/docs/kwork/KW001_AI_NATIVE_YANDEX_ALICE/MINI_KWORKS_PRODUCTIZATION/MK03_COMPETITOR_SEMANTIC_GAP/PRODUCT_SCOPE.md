# MK03 — PRODUCT SCOPE

Status: **PHASE 0 PASS / V1 SCOPE FROZEN FOR METHOD DEVELOPMENT**

## 1. Client-facing product promise

MK03 answers a bounded competitive-search question:

> Which sites actually compete with the client in Yandex organic search, what additional in-scope demand/topics their visible pages expose, and which of those directions are confirmed gaps or opportunities for the client.

The product is evidence-first and Yandex-only.

## 2. Positive sold result

The client receives:

1. a register of real Yandex organic competitors discovered from representative in-scope query families;
2. the competitor pages that actually appeared in the discovery Search evidence;
3. competitor-derived topic/seed candidates with traceable source page/topic lineage;
4. Wordstat demand validation for genuinely new candidate directions;
5. current exact-query Yandex visibility evidence where an exact competitor visibility statement is made;
6. comparison against the client's current public-site/semantic coverage;
7. classified gap decisions such as confirmed missed demand, already covered, off-scope, or evidence-required;
8. bounded structural/page-type opportunity notes where evidence supports them;
9. a client-readable summary explaining what was found and what should be considered next.

## 3. Hard distinctions

```text
BUSINESS COMPETITOR != ORGANIC SEARCH COMPETITOR
COMPETITOR PAGE TOPIC != EXACT QUERY RANKING
COMPETITOR-DERIVED SEED != ACCEPTED KEYWORD
WORDSTAT DEMAND != CLIENT BUSINESS FIT
COMPETITOR VISIBILITY != AUTOMATIC GAP
GAP != AUTOMATIC NEW PAGE
STRUCTURAL OPPORTUNITY != FULL TARGET ARCHITECTURE
TESTED QUERY VISIBILITY != FULL DOMAIN KEYWORD UNIVERSE
```

## 4. V1 operating mode

Supported V1 mode: **existing public site**.

The current public site is inspected as part of the work. The client is not required to restate all products/services/directions already visible on the site.

If the site leaves a material business ambiguity, preserve it and request one concrete clarification rather than inventing business truth.

## 5. Yandex-only evidence model

Base evidence may include:

- current regional ordinary Yandex Search;
- Yandex Wordstat;
- public pages of the client site;
- public pages of competitors that bear material evidence;
- preserved current project evidence when still valid and reusable.

Optional private Yandex evidence may be used only if separately supplied/authorized and material to a named decision.

## 6. Search-competitor discovery boundary

Client-supplied competitor names are optional hints.

A domain becomes an MK03 Search competitor only through current Yandex evidence in the target region for in-scope query families.

Competitors may be classified for analytical routing as direct business competitor, other organic model, aggregator/directory, marketplace, manufacturer/brand source, informational publisher, or other review class. This is project taxonomy, not Yandex taxonomy.

No universal competitor-count threshold is frozen. Coverage expands only while additional domains/pages add material new information.

## 7. Competitor-page boundary

Prefer competitor URLs that actually appeared in current Yandex results used for discovery.

Inspect only public elements needed for competitor-derived demand/gap discovery, such as page type, Title/H1 where available, topic/product/service/use-case axes, material headings and navigation directions.

The base product does not require a deep backlink/technical/conversion competitor audit.

## 8. Gap decision contract

Every material candidate should end in an explicit state equivalent to:

```text
CONFIRMED_GAP
ALREADY_COVERED
REJECT_OFF_SCOPE
HOLD_EVIDENCE
```

`CONFIRMED_GAP` requires sufficient demand, business fit and evidence for the claim being made.

A confirmed semantic gap may support a structural/page-type opportunity note. It does not automatically authorize a new URL, site architecture, copywriting brief or implementation ticket.

## 9. Neighbouring-product boundaries

MK03 remains distinct from:

- MK01: standalone full semantic-core + clustering product;
- MK02: full phrase→page mapping, target SEO architecture and page-by-page TZ;
- MK04: dedicated query→page/intents/cannibalization product;
- MK05: implementation-ready SEO TZ from accepted decisions;
- MK06: Alice/Yandex Neuro/AEO diagnostic;
- MK07: full applicable KW-001 complex.

MK03 can output accepted gap phrases/directions as its own result, but it does not silently give away the complete downstream products above.

## 10. Client-result usefulness gate

Without repository knowledge, the recipient must be able to answer:

1. Which competitors were actually found in Yandex and for which search families?
2. Which competitor pages produced new candidate directions?
3. What exactly was new versus already covered?
4. Which candidate demand is supported by Wordstat?
5. Which exact query→competitor observations are Search-tested versus inferred from page topics?
6. Which gaps are confirmed, rejected or held?
7. What structural/content opportunity does each confirmed gap suggest, if any?
8. What remains uncertain and what evidence would resolve it?

Summary counts without inspectable competitor/gap evidence are not sufficient.
