# MK03 — MARKET REALITY

Date: 2026-09-11
Status: **PHASE 1 PASS / FRESH MARKET BASELINE**

## Conclusion

There is clear current demand for competitor-based SEO/semantic research, but the market contains different products that must not be conflated:

1. cheap competitor-keyword exports / semantic dumps;
2. semantic-core services that use competitors as one source;
3. broader competitor audits including traffic, backlinks, commercial factors and conversion review;
4. jobs specifically asking to find competitors, collect competitor semantics and identify missing page/structure opportunities.

MK03 is positioned as an evidence-backed Yandex competitor-gap product, not a raw export and not a full technical/backlink/business competitor audit.
