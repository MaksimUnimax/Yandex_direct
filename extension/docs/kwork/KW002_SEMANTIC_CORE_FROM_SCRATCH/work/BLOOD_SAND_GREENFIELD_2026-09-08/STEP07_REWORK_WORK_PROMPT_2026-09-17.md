### 5.1 Valid candidate characteristics

A candidate may be retained when the wording itself represents a compact, reproducible semantic object/direction such as:

- a recognized term/name/symbol;
- category/subcategory;
- product/service naming direction;
- audience/use-case formulation;
- material/attribute/form factor;
- spelling/transliteration/naming variant;
- stable problem/service formulation;
- stable informational question/topic formulation.

It must make sense independently of the surrounding source sentence.

### 5.2 Raw prose is provenance, not automatically candidate identity

The following MUST NOT automatically become `NEW_CANDIDATE` merely because a product/amulet/rune-related word appears:

```text
literary quotation / dialogue line
sentence corpus/example sentence
book/author/year citation
bibliographic metadata
broken etymology fragment
truncated punctuation fragment
generic UI text
"вам также может быть интересно"
"о книге"
"примечания"
button/navigation boilerplate without independent topical meaning
incidental object mention inside unrelated prose
unrelated article/product theme
```

Map them to the correct audit state/reason (`OUT_OF_SCOPE`, UI/noise, foreign/unrelated, or `AMBIGUOUS`) unless a valid separately derived candidate exists.

### 5.3 Long body text may produce a derived candidate only under a strict reproducible rule

A long sentence/paragraph may yield a candidate only if:

1. a concise semantic span/formulation is explicitly present or can be derived by a minimal reproducible transformation;
2. the derived wording is independently meaningful;
3. the exact raw source text remains in provenance;
4. `transformation_rule` and detail state exactly how the candidate was derived;
5. no new business/search-demand claim is invented.

Use schema-supported transformation states such as `LONG_FORM_SPAN_DERIVATION` where appropriate.

Do not transform arbitrary prose into a desired keyword merely because it sounds useful.

### 5.4 Informational/dictionary pages are not globally forbidden

Do NOT overcorrect by excluding every dictionary/informational page.

A heading/topic such as a genuine meaning/etymology/synonym/use-case formulation can be a valid informational candidate if it is compact, in scope and independently meaningful.

The defect is **arbitrary text-as-candidate**, not informational content itself.

### 5.5 Claim boundary

Mystical/symbolic/health/protective wording may be evidence of what users/competitors discuss. It is NOT accepted as a true physical/medical/business efficacy claim.

```text
COMPETITOR CLAIM LANGUAGE != CLIENT FACT
COMPETITOR CLAIM LANGUAGE != PROVEN SEARCH DEMAND
```

Keep the semantic wording only when it is a valid candidate direction; do not certify the claim as true.
