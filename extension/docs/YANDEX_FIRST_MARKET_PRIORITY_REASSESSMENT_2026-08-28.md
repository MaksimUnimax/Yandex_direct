## 1. Decision question

After Phases 0–8 and the accepted O-001 / GenSearch work, determine the next highest-leverage engineering/research target under the permanent worker model:

```text
ChatGPT Plus = reasoning / analysis / semantic architecture / recommendations / artifacts / QA
Yandex Marketing Bridge = controlled authenticated provider hands
Human owner/operator = authorization and irreducible local/live boundary
```

The question is not whether Google is useful in the abstract. The question is whether a Google hand is the best next investment compared with improving the existing Yandex hands or adding a crawler/site-inventory hand for the Russian-market commercial workflow.

## 8. Google re-entry triggers

Google remains backlog, not rejected permanently.

Re-open Google production priority if any of these occurs:

```text
A. next 30 independently sampled real marketplace jobs show >=25% of otherwise attractive jobs blocked specifically by missing Google evidence;
B. Google share of the target Russian search market rises materially above the current ~25% and remains there;
C. a concrete high-ticket repeatable service requires Google data often enough to dominate Direct/Metrika/crawler opportunity cost;
D. an official/stable/low-friction provider route appears that materially reduces implementation/operator burden.
```

Until a trigger is met, no Google OAuth/client/store/identity work is justified.
