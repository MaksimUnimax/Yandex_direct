All observations below are direct ordinary Search provider measurements for region `225`.

Top-10 composition:

1. Ozon — marketplace/category.
2. `slavyanskieoberegi.ru` — specialist independent.
3. Wildberries — marketplace/category.
4. `simvolroda.ru` — specialist independent.
5. `slavyanskieoberegi.ru` — catalog.

8. `slavyanskieoberegi.ru` — product category.
9. `ruyan-master.ru` — specialist shop.
10. `veles.bz` — commercial category.

Observed pattern: commercial/category-first; marketplaces hold #1 and #3 but specialist independent sites occupy most of the Top-10. One specialist domain occupies #2/#5/#6/#8.

### 5.2 `печать велеса`

Top-10 composition:

1. Wildberries.
2. specialist independent page.
3–5. Yandex Market results.
6. Avito.
7. editorial meaning article.
8. Ozon.
9. Livemaster.
10. Yandex Market.

Observed pattern: 8/10 results are trading platforms/marketplaces; one specialist independent site ranks #2; one clean editorial meaning result ranks #7. Strong product/transactional composition.

### 5.3 `оберег в машину`

Top-10 includes marketplace results, independent product pages, a choice guide, community posts and other informational results. The result set spans Orthodox icons, a car-house-spirit product, esoteric symbols and other protection/use-case products.

Observed pattern: mixed commercial + choice/use-case; the query is broader than any one symbol.

### 5.4 `подвеска на зеркало в машину`

All 10 observed Top-10 results are commercial marketplace/platform/catalog pages: Ozon, Wildberries, Avito, AliExpress, Yandex Market, Livemaster and similar.

Observed pattern: near-pure form-factor/product transaction. The results include toys, JDM accessories, religious symbols, gifts, handmade items and amulets; the query is not semantically limited to protection symbols.

### 5.5 `вегвизир`

Observed Top-10: 7 commercial/platform results + 3 informational/reference results.

Observed pattern: mixed entity + commerce. Commercial demand is strong even without a purchase modifier, while ordinary Search also returns definition/history content.

### 5.6 `талисман знак зодиака`

Observed Top-10 is approximately 6 informational/guide results vs 4 commercial results. Stones/jewelry content is a major neighboring intent.

Observed pattern: guide/selection-first; strong semantic contamination for direct demand for this specific pendant category.

### 5.7 `алатырь оберег`

Observed pattern: commercial-first with a visible meaning/support layer. A specialist independent site appears in the Top-5. The #1 Wildberries snippet directly contained a product from the project brand: `Кровь и Песок / Славянский оберег в машину "Алатырь (Крест Сварога)"`, with 593 ratings at capture time. This is marketplace visibility, not visibility of an independent site.

### 5.8 `оберег велес`

Observed pattern: commercial-first with strong niche independent competition. Specialist commerce sites are represented more strongly than for `печать велеса`; an informational result and a hybrid meaning/product page are also present.

### 5.9 `подарок мужчине в машину`

Observed pattern: almost entirely shopping/gift SERP. Practical automotive gifts dominate: kits, car-care products, holders and general accessories. A symbolic pendant is not the dominant default answer.

### 5.10 `подарок автомобилисту`

Observed pattern: commercial catalogs/platforms overwhelmingly dominate with one strong editorial ideas result. A Wildberries snippet showed a pendant as one gift option, but not as the dominant category answer.

### 6.4 `вегвизир значение`

The observed Top-10 is overwhelmingly definition/history/meaning material: specialist editorial pages, reference material, community explanations and related meaning content. Rank #1 is a specialist editorial page.

Observed pattern: clean meaning/definition intent, sharply different from the mixed commercial primary `вегвизир` query.

### 6.5 `шлем ужаса оберег`

The observed Top-10 is commerce-heavy: Wildberries #1, Ozon #2, specialist content/commerce #3, Yandex Market and Livemaster results, with supporting history/meaning material also present.

Observed pattern: commercial-first symbol query with a secondary meaning/history layer.

## 7. Baseline tensions the analyst must resolve independently

These are questions, not conclusions:

1. Should marketplace-heavy primary symbol roots and meaning-specific variants share one page job or be split?
2. Should automotive protection/use-case roots and mirror-pendant form-factor roots share one target or be separated?
3. How should a high-volume but semantically contaminated zodiac family be treated?
4. Does large generic automotive gift demand deserve a page when direct fit to the actual assortment is weak?
5. Which symbol families justify specialist meaning/support content in addition to commerce?
6. Where does ordinary Search show a realistic specialist-independent-site opportunity rather than an almost pure platform battle?
7. Which low-volume exact `symbol + car` roots should be absorbed into stronger use-case/form clusters rather than treated as standalone targets?

The independent Pass A analyst must answer these from the packet only and must record uncertainty where the evidence is insufficient.
